What you do

Fight turn by turn

Fights play out on a grid. Your weapon sets your style: melee, ranged or magic, each with its own action bar.

Weapons that feel different

Daggers bleed, maces crit, crossbows stun, bows loose extra arrows, and elemental staves each add a perk of their own.

Train, craft, trade

Gather, craft and find gear, fill your bag and bank, and shop with the people of the Reach.

Share the fields

Other players are out there with you. Chat, and watch the fields refill faster when the realm is busy.

How to start

  1. Press Create account, pick a username and a password, and say you are 13 or older.
  2. Save the recovery key the game shows you. It is the way back in if you forget your password.
  3. Pick your hero's look, then walk out and hit the first thing that moves.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. The game is free to play.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in a modern browser on desktop, tablet or phone. Tick "Keep me signed in" on the login page and you will not need to type your password again for up to 30 days.

I lost my password.

Use "Forgot password?" on the login page with the recovery key you saved when you made the account.

Is it online with other people?

Yes. The server is small for now, so if it is full or offline, try again a little later.
