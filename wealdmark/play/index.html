Party

A party holds up to four heroes. When one of you starts a fight, partners within eight tiles join it, and iron coins are shared by damage dealt.

Mercenaries

Hire one in a town for gold coins. It fights beside you for five fights and counts as half a hero when monsters scale. If it is knocked out, it leaves. You earn XP only from your own damage.

Dungeon

Stand near a dungeon gate. Each hero pays the fee once per run, and the run is a private copy of the dungeon. Walking out keeps what you found.
