Heroes of the Wealdmark

A free pixel RPG that runs in your browser.

The Crown is gone and nobody tells you where to go. Walk out of town, pick a fight with whatever is standing in the grass, and see how far your hero gets. Fights run in real time, there is nothing to download, and nobody can pay for a stronger hero.

Checking the realm...

Monsters wait in the open

There are no loading screens between the road and the fight. Click a monster to start one, or Shift-click to read about it first. Your abilities sit on an action bar and come back after a cooldown, and auto combat takes over when you want to sit back.

Over 30 monsters and bosses live in the Reach, from imps and wolves to quarry golems and salt crabs.

If you lose, you drop some iron bits. The share grows with your level. Your gear and skills stay with you.

Towns are where you regroup

Talk to the people of the Reach and take on their quests. Shop, use the bank, craft, gather, and sort out your bag without watching your back, because towns are safe.

Other heroes share the world with you. Monsters come back faster the busier the realm is.

No pay to win. Ever.

Gold, gear, levels and loot come from playing, and they come the same way for everyone. If we ever sell something, it will only change how your hero looks. There are no loot boxes and no paid shortcuts past the grind.

It works on a phone

Same game, same account. Tap to walk, tap a monster to fight, and drag the view to look ahead. A Back to hero button brings you home when you are done looking.

Start in a minute

  1. Create an account. Pick a username and a password, and confirm you are 13 or older.
  2. Save your recovery key. The game shows it once. It is how you get back in if you forget your password.
  3. Choose your look and walk out. Click a tile to move, click a monster to fight it.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. The game is free to play and there is no pay to win. Anything we sell later will only change how your hero looks.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in a modern browser on desktop, tablet or phone. Tick "Keep me signed in" at login and you will not need your password again for up to 30 days.

I lost my password.

Use "Forgot password?" on the login page, with the recovery key the game showed you when you made the account.

What happens when I lose a fight?

You lose some iron bits. The share grows with your level. Your gear and skills are never touched.

Who else is playing?

Other heroes, on a small server. If the realm shows as full or closed above, try again a little later.

Make music or art? Help build the Wealdmark

We need musicians for the towns and the wilds, and artists for characters, gear, pets and places.

This is unpaid and credit-only. You get your name in the game's credits and your work in front of every player.
