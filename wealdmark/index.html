No pay to win. Ever.

Nobody can buy a stronger hero. Gold, gear, levels and loot come from playing, and they come the same way for everyone. If we ever sell anything, it will only change how your hero looks. No loot boxes, no paid power, no paid shortcuts past the grind.

Every weapon plays differently

Your weapon decides your fighting style and your action bar: melee, ranged or magic. Switch weapons and the bar switches with it. Each weapon also carries a perk of its own.

SwordHits 5% harder.
DaggerTwo actions a turn. Wounds bleed.
Mace10% chance to crit.
SpearTwo-handed. Blocks 20% of attacks.
BowLonger reach, extra crit chance, sometimes two arrows.
CrossbowCan stun an enemy for three rounds.
PistolOne-handed. Wield two. May finish a wounded enemy outright.
StavesAir dodges, fire burns hotter, water heals, earth shrugs off blows.

A world to work in

Gather, craft and trade. Fill your bag and your bank. Take on quests from the people of the Reach, and meet other heroes in the fields, where monsters come back faster the busier the realm gets.

Get started in a minute

  1. Create an account. Pick a username and a password, and confirm you are 13 or older.
  2. Save your recovery key. The game shows it once. It is the way back in if you forget your password.
  3. Choose your look and walk out. Click a tile to move, click a monster to fight it.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. The game is free to play, and there is no pay to win. Anything we sell later will be cosmetic only.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in a modern browser on desktop, tablet or phone. Tick "Keep me signed in" at login and you will not need your password again for up to 30 days.

I lost my password.

Use "Forgot password?" on the login page, with the recovery key you saved when you made the account.

Who else is playing?

Other heroes, on a small server. If the realm shows as full or offline above, try again a little later.

Want to be part of this?

We are looking for musicians and artists to help build the Wealdmark: music for the towns and the wilds, and art for characters, gear, pets and places.

This is an unpaid, credit-only role. There is no pay. You get your name in the game's credits and your work shown to every player.
