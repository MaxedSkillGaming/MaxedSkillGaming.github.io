What you can do

Fight

Real-time combat with an action bar, cooldowns and auto combat. Bosses mark the ground under you, so keep moving. Dungeons run three floors deep.

Gather and craft

Twelve trades: forestry, mining, fishing, hunting, herbalism, farming, excavation, smithing, cooking, crafting, alchemy and jewelcrafting.

Explore

Attune waystones and travel between them. Skill-gated shortcuts link far regions, and some you can build yourself.

Quest

Take on 76 quests from the people of the Reach, and earn gold, titles and gear for finishing them.

Collect

Gear comes in rarities with rolled mods. Fill your bestiary, take a companion along and wear what you earn.

Play together

One shared online world. Other heroes work, fight and trade alongside you.

Real-time combat

Fights begin in the open world with no loading screens. Click a monster to engage it, or Shift-click to inspect it first. Abilities sit on an action bar with cooldowns, and auto combat is available when you prefer it.

The world holds 88 monsters and bosses, from imps and wolves to quarry golems and salt crabs.

Towns and quests

Speak with the people of the Reach and take on their quests. Towns are safe places to shop, bank, craft and manage your inventory.

Other heroes share the world with you.

Fair progression

Gold, gear, levels and loot are earned through play, in the same way for every player.

Play on any device

The same game and account work on desktop, tablet and phone, with touch controls for walking, fighting and moving the view.

Getting started

  1. Create an account. Pick a username and a password, and confirm you are 13 or older.
  2. Save your recovery key. The game shows it once. It is how you get back in if you forget your password.
  3. Choose your look and walk out. Click a tile to move, click a monster to fight it.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Heroes of the Wealdmark is free to play.

Do I need to download anything?

Not for the open beta. You can play on desktop, tablet or phone. Tick "Keep me signed in" at login and you will not need your password again for up to 30 days.

I lost my password.

Use "Forgot password?" on the login page, with the recovery key the game showed you when you made the account.

Can I play with others?

Yes. The Reach is a shared online world, and other heroes play alongside you.

Does donating give me anything in the game?

No. Donations are a thank-you and cover hosting. Gold, gear and levels are earned through play for everyone.

Support the Wealdmark

The game is free and stays free. If you enjoy it, a donation helps pay for hosting and keeps new regions, quests and music coming.

Donations are a thank-you. They do not give gold, gear, levels or any other advantage in the game.
