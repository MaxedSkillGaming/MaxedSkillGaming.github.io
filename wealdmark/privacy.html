Privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026. Publisher: Eternity Squared Technologies LLC, United States, trading as Maxed Skill Gaming. Contact: hello@eternitysquaredtech.com.

What we collect

  • Account data: your username, a hashed password, and an optional email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID and email.
  • Game data: your characters, items, skills, quest progress and teleport waystones, plus chat and trade messages you send in the game.
  • Technical data: your IP address, browser type and session tokens. We use these to sign you in, limit abuse and keep you connected.

We do not run advertising, analytics or tracking, and we do not sell your data.

Cookies and browser storage

This website sets no cookies. The game stores a few items in your browser's local storage so it can work: your settings (zoom, chat opacity, interface choices), which hints you have seen, and, if you tick "Keep me signed in", a sign-in token that lets you skip your password for up to 30 days. These items are needed for features you ask for, so we do not show a consent banner. You can clear them any time in your browser settings, or by logging out.

Fonts

The fonts on this site are served from our own pages. Your browser does not contact Google or any other font service.

Donations

If you donate, you pay on a page run by Stripe. Stripe collects and processes your payment details under its own privacy policy. We do not see or store your card number. We receive the amount and the contact details Stripe passes on, such as your name and email, so we can keep records and answer questions. Stripe sets its own cookies on its pages. A donation does not give you anything in the game, and it is not a charitable gift, so it is not tax deductible.

Why we use your data

To run your account, save your progress, prevent cheating and abuse, keep our records for donations, and fix bugs. Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are performing our agreement with you, our legitimate interest in running a safe service, and our legal duties.

Where it is stored and who handles it

The game servers are run by us in the United States. Traffic to them goes through Cloudflare, which sees your IP address while passing requests along. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs. Stripe handles donations. If you use Google sign-in, Google handles that sign-in. Your data may be processed in the United States, which can be outside your own country.

How long we keep it

We keep account and game data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your data within 30 days, except for what we must keep for legal, tax or abuse-prevention reasons. Donation records are kept as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data by writing to hello@eternitysquaredtech.com. If you live in the EU, the UK or California, you have further rights under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA, including the right to object to processing and to complain to your data protection authority. We do not sell or share personal data for advertising.

Children

The game is not for children under 13, and in some countries the minimum age is higher. We do not knowingly collect data from children below the age that applies where they live. If you think a child has made an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

We post changes on this page and update the date at the top.
